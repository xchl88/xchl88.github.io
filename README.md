# Liang-Yu Lai Casebook

Source of https://xchl88.github.io/ : four investment cases (a £250m NAV facility against HgCapital Trust's fund interests, TeamSystem leveraged credit, a Beatrice offshore wind stake priced against NAV, and research on portfolio dependence), plus method and disclosure.

The HgCapital Trust, TeamSystem and Beatrice cases are independent analyses of public information; the dependence case is a UCL MSc dissertation. Nothing here is investment advice.
